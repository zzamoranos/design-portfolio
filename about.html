A bit about me

Lovely to meet you! I’m Zeaj (pronounced zé-yuh), a product designer tinkering with interfaces and code, and a lover of life. I design products guided by intention and human connection that make people happy ⊹ ࣪ ˖

Design has always been a big part of my life, but curiosity has pulled me in lots of directions: graphic design, architecture, visual arts, videography, and product design. What I love most about it is following an idea as it grows into something real that resonates with people.

Open to full-time, new grad design opportunities. If you’re interested in working together, let’s chat!

My design ethos

  1. 01

    Curiosity first

    Good design starts with questions. Curiosity helps me see problems from new angles and raises the bar on craft.

  2. 02

    Less, but better

    A design succeeds when it helps people reach their goals. The path there should be simple and delightful, not full of noise.

  3. 03

    Sweat the details

    Small, thoughtful details and micro-interactions that create moments of joy people remember.
